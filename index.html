HOW IT WORKS

Each guild member's data — name, role, skills, tier — deterministically generates a unique monster card. The same member always produces the same monster. No artist needed. The algorithm IS the artist.

Every card has scannable barcodes encoding the card's identity, element, and rarity across three data channels. Scan with any barcode reader for the card ID, or use the Barcode Monsters app to decode all three channels.

Every monster has a unique 12×12×12 voxel model that can be exported as STL and 3D-printed at 24mm × 24mm × 24mm. ~15 minute print, ~2g PLA, ~$0.10 per model.
