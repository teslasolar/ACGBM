# BARCODE MONSTERS — Official Rules v1.0

> Cards Are Barcodes. Models Are Printable. Monsters Are Members.

---

## Overview

Barcode Monsters is a trading card game where every card is a scannable barcode,
every monster is generated from real guild member data, and every card has a
unique 3D-printable STL model. No borrowed IP. The DATA designs the monsters.

---

## Setup

- **Players:** 2
- **Deck Size:** 20 cards per player
- **Starting Hand:** 5 cards (draw from deck)
- **Starting HP:** 8,000
- **Starting Energy:** 2⚡
- **Win Condition:** Reduce opponent HP to 0

---

## Turn Structure

Each turn follows 5 phases:

### 1. DRAW PHASE
Draw 1 card from your deck. If your deck is empty, you take 500 damage instead.

### 2. PLAY PHASE
Play up to 2 monster cards from your hand to your field (max 5 on field).
Each card costs energy equal to its tier:
- Tier 1 = 1⚡
- Tier 2 = 2⚡
- Tier 3 = 3⚡

LIGHTNING element cards cost **double** energy but can attack all opponents.

### 3. ABILITY PHASE
Activate 1 ability from a monster you control. Abilities cost ⚡ as listed.

### 4. BATTLE PHASE
Attack with 1 monster. Choose an opponent's monster or attack directly.

**Monster vs Monster:**
- Attacker ATK vs Defender DEF
- If ATK > DEF: damage = (ATK - DEF) → opponent loses that HP
- If DEF ≥ ATK: recoil = (DEF - ATK) → attacker's controller loses HP

**Direct Attack:** (if opponent has no monsters)
- Full ATK value → opponent HP

### 5. END PHASE
Gain 1⚡ (max 10⚡). Turn passes to opponent.

## Element System

| Element   | Strong Against | Weak Against |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| FIRE      | METAL         | PRISM        |
| METAL     | WIND          | FIRE         |
| WIND      | CRYSTAL       | METAL        |
| CRYSTAL   | VOID          | WIND         |
| VOID      | PRISM         | CRYSTAL      |
| PRISM     | FIRE          | VOID         |

**Special Elements:**
- **LIGHTNING:** Advantage vs ALL elements. Costs double ⚡.
- **AETHER:** No advantage, but draws 2 extra cards when played.

**Element Bonus:** +500 ATK when attacking a weaker element.
**Element Penalty:** -500 ATK when attacking a stronger element.

## Card Stats

| Stat | Meaning | Derived From |
|------|---------|-------------|
| ATK  | Attack Power | Offensive skills × tier |
| DEF  | Defense Power | Defensive skills × tier |
| SPD  | Speed | Activity rate |
| RES  | Resilience | Years active × tier |

**Total Power** = ATK + DEF + SPD + RES

## Evolution

If a monster stays on the field for **3 consecutive turns**, it evolves:
- All stats increase by **+20%**
- Visual model updates (if viewing digitally)
- Flip to evolved side (physical cards: use evolution overlay)

---

## Special Rules

### Barcode Verification
In physical play, scan your opponent's card barcode to verify authenticity.
If a card fails verification → the player using it automatically loses.

### 3D Print Bonus
If you physically 3D-printed the monster and have the model at the table:
**+200 ATK bonus** (honor system / photo proof).

### Deck Building
- Maximum 2 copies of any card in a deck
- Must include at least 3 different elements
- LEGENDARY cards: maximum 1 per deck

## Quick Reference

```
TURN: Draw → Play (2 max) → Ability (1) → Attack (1) → End (+1⚡)
COMBAT: ATK vs DEF. Difference = damage to loser's controller.
ELEMENT: Rock-paper-scissors ring + LIGHTNING (beats all, 2x cost) + AETHER (draws 2)
EVOLVE: 3 turns on field → +20% all stats
WIN: Opponent HP reaches 0
```
